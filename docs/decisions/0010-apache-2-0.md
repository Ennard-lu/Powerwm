# ADR-0010: Apache License 2.0

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-24
- Owners: powerwm maintainers
- Related: [README.md](../../README.md), [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md), [ADR-0005](0005-license.md)

## Context

ADR-0005 listed MIT, Apache-2.0, dual licensing, GPL, and LGPL without choosing. The project
now selects Apache-2.0.

## Decision

1. Project license: Apache License 2.0.
2. Add the full `LICENSE` text.
3. Source files use `SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0`.
4. Contributions are accepted under Apache-2.0 without a CLA.
5. Third-party code/XML keeps its original license and notices; verify compatibility.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Permissive: commercial use, modification, closed distribution allowed.
- Explicit patent grant and contribution terms.
- Compatible with much of the Linux/Wayland ecosystem.
- SPDX enables automated auditing.

### Negative

- Does not require downstream reciprocity.
- Copyright/license notices and NOTICE (if present) must be preserved.
- GPLv2-only compatibility needs case-by-case review.

## Alternatives

| Option | Pros | Cons | Why not |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MIT | minimal | no patent grant | weaker protection |
| MIT OR Apache-2.0 | flexible | dual-license management | Apache alone suffices |
| GPL-3.0 | reciprocity | adoption limits | conflicts with goals |
| LGPL-3.0 | library-friendly | compositor questions | poor fit |

## Revisit conditions

- The project decides reciprocity is mandatory.
- A critical dependency is incompatible with Apache-2.0 and cannot be isolated.
